It started at a church Christmas service

Hawaii Booths began with a simple idea and an extraordinary moment — a need for a touch of magic at a Christmas service hosted by a local church. What started as a DIY project for that one event grew into the service we run today.

We believe photography is not just about images. It is about encapsulating the emotions, stories, and connections that make life special. That belief shapes how we show up: on time, set up early, and running the booth ourselves so nobody at your event has to think about it.
